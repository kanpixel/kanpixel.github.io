Description

Card Rush is a strategy auto-battler about one question: who moves first? Buy cards in the shop, place them on a 3×3 board and send them into battle. Fights play out on their own — every card has ATK, HP and SPD, and a turn gauge fills by SPD: a card twice as fast acts twice as often. Melee cards strike from the front of their lane, ranged cards from anywhere, and every tribe has a formation that lights up only in the right shape.

Each run is 10 wins before 5 lives are gone, against saved boards from other players' runs. Between runs, 20 Masters, a mastery track and a vault of relics and talismans let you grow; the Ascension ladder and Heat push the challenge, ranked Arena seasons and a daily Boss Hunt give you something to chase.

Features

  • 10 tribes, 100 cards — Fire burns, Stone endures, Wind strikes first, Time bends the turn gauge — each tribe plays its own way, every card has two upgrade paths.
  • Speed decides — ATK, HP and SPD: a turn gauge fills by speed, so a card twice as fast acts twice as often. Placement on the 3×3 board matters.
  • 20 Masters — Each with their own passives and a mastery track that grows as you clear harder runs.
  • 11 bosses — Bosses break into your runs with their own move sets, phases and arena rules.
  • Ranked Arena seasons — Climb the leaderboard against other players' saved boards, season after season.
  • Daily Boss Hunt — 10 bosses × 10 levels, the same enemy side for everyone — post the fastest clear.
  • A relic vault — 134 card relics and 56 talismans: merge them, star them up, raise them to +10.
